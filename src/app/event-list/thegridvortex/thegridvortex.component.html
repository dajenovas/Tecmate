The Grid Vortex

Gear up for Grid Vortex – a high-energy tech challenge designed to test your teamwork, creativity and web designing skills. Participants will face two dynamic rounds, each focusing on different tech abilities.

Event Structure

Round 1 : Quantum Link

The first stage of GridVortex focuses on teamwork, quick thinking, and recognizing technology concepts through visual clues. Teams will identify the connection between a series of tech-related images, testing their collaboration and reasoning under time pressure.

Round Format

  • Mode : Interactive Task
  • Team Size : Max. 2 Members per team
  • Duration : 15 Minutes
  • Focus Areas: Technology Recognition, Logical Reasoning, Connection Solving

Rules & Guidelines

  • Each team will identify the connection between the given technology-related images.
  • The round will consist only of connection-based tasks.
  • Images will be related to technology topics such as software, hardware, tools, or tech companies.
  • Teams must solve as many connections as possible within the time limit.
  • Accuracy and teamwork are key – incorrect or incomplete answers may reduce scores.
  • Top-performing teams will qualify for Round 2

Round 2 : Webscaping

The grand finale of GridVortex – a 45-minute challenge to create a fully functional webpage. Teams must develop a front page, login page, and main page, and present their work. Creativity, functionality, and presentation skills will determine the winners.

Round Format

  • Mode : Practical Web Development
  • Duration : 45 Minutes
  • Task : Design and implement a webpage with:
    • Front Page
    • Login Page
    • Main Page
  • Presentation : Each team must present their webpage after completion.

Rules & Guidelines

  • All webpage elements should be functional and visually appealing
  • Teams must submit their work and perform a live presentation.
  • Use of pre-built templates is allowed, but originality will be scored.
  • Judges’ decision is final in case of tie-breakers.

The team demonstrating the best combination of speed, accuracy, problem-solving, web development, and presentation skills will be crowned GridVortex Champions. Top three teams receive prizes, and all participants earn certificates.
